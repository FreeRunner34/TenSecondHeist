Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026.

Data on your device

The app saves routes, progress, settings, and introductory assistance tokens locally. It has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, or in-game uploads. Local progress and tokens may be lost on uninstall unless recovered through a device backup.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Full Campaign in-app purchase under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. The app checks the StoreKit entitlement to unlock paid levels and offers Restore Purchases. Optional token packs are not sold.

Replay sharing

Replays are rendered from the in-game simulation on your device. The app does not record unrelated device screens or notifications, and it never uploads a replay. Sharing occurs only if you choose an option in the iOS share sheet.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the support page. Public issues should never contain private information.
