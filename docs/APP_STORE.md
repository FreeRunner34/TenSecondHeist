### Description

Ten seconds to get in and out. All the time you need to plan.

Draw routes for your crew, time each move, and press GO to see the heist unfold. A camera catches the thief? Rewind, inspect the exact moment, adjust the plan, and try again. Replays and retries are always free.

- Coordinate a thief, hacker, and decoy across handcrafted floor plans.
- Learn predictable guard patrols, camera sweeps, timed shutdowns, and distractions.
- Play eight free operations, with forty more in the optional permanent Full Campaign purchase.
- Aim for Perfect Heist by meeting the action target without assistance.
- Use three included Inside Help tokens for optional level clues or extended camera shutdowns.
- Export a replay to preview or share when you choose.

No ads, subscriptions, random rewards, or required account. Gameplay works offline after installation. The optional Full Campaign purchase needs access to the App Store. Inside Help token packs are not currently sold.

## App Review notes draft

The app opens directly with no login. Tap Start Heist to access the first eight levels. In a level, select a crew member, drag a route across walkable tiles or use STEP arrows, add TAKE/HACK/DISTRACT/WAIT actions when available, and press GO. Rewind, retry, and pause are free. From the home screen, Full Campaign opens the non-consumable in-app purchase for levels 9–48. Restore Purchases is available on the store and in Settings. Three introductory Inside Help tokens are local to the installation. Optional paid packs require iCloud and a connection to purchase, spend, and recover across devices using the same iCloud account. The app has no user-generated content, account deletion flow, advertisements, or in-app sharing feed. Replay export uses only a rendered game simulation and opens the system share sheet after a preview.
