Ten Second Heist

Support for the iPhone tactical puzzle game.

Need help?

Open a support issue and include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. Issues are public. Do not include passwords, payment details, or other private information.

Purchases

The Full Campaign is one permanent purchase. In the app, open Settings → Restore Full Campaign while signed into the Apple Account that made the purchase. The three introductory Inside Help tokens are local to the app install; token packs are not sold.
